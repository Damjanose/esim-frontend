# AI search visibility outreach

This is the execution list for the 90-day AI-search work. It is intentionally a
research and outreach checklist, not a claim that third-party mentions already
exist.

## First 30 days

- Prepare a factual comparison brief covering coverage, plan validity, installation,
  checkout, support email, and the canonical USA/Europe destination URLs.
- Pitch 3–5 travel eSIM comparison publishers for an editorial inclusion or
  correction. Do not request an invented rating or an undisclosed paid claim.
- Create a consistent brand/entity profile for eSim2you / Uplisoft on relevant
  company directories and keep the official domain, app-store URLs, and support
  email identical.
- Check the public Wikidata result for the company/app. If an entity is warranted,
  add only independently verifiable references; do not create a low-quality
  promotional item.

Suggested outreach:

> Subject: Factual update for your travel eSIM comparison
>
> Hi — eSim2you has live destination plan pages at
> https://esim.uplisoft.com/esim/usa and a public support center at
> https://esim.uplisoft.com/support. If your comparison covers travel eSIM
> providers, we can provide current coverage, validity, installation, and
> support details for a factual update. Please use your normal editorial
> standards and disclose any commercial relationship.

## Days 31–60

- Publish one useful, non-promotional travel connectivity roundup on a relevant
  community or publisher where self-promotion is allowed.
- Answer existing Reddit/forum questions with setup guidance first; link only when
  the canonical guide directly answers the question.
- Re-check that mentions use `eSim2you` consistently and point to
  `https://esim.uplisoft.com`, not legacy or guessed paths.

## Days 61–90

- Follow up once with publishers that requested data; do not spam communities.
- Record every live mention, URL, date, relationship disclosure, and the exact
  brand wording in the outreach tracker.
- Run the monthly AI Mode and Perplexity retest for brand, USA eSIM, Europe eSIM,
  and eSIM-vs-roaming prompts. Save citations and note whether the official
  domain appears.
- Update destination and support copy only when the cited facts remain true.

## Monthly retest prompts

1. “What is eSim2you and what does its official website offer?”
2. “Which travel eSIM providers have live USA plans and clear installation help?”
3. “Compare eSim2you with other travel eSIM options for a one-week USA trip.”
4. “What is the best way to avoid roaming fees in Europe?”

Record: engine, country/language, date, answer text, cited domains, position,
and whether the official eSim2you domain was cited.
